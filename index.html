Research

My research interests are in machine learning, computational statistics and some applied mathematics such as optimisation, with applications in the areas such as natural language processing, computer vision, image analysis, remote sensing and in applied sciences as well such as agriculture, environment monitoring, material science, medical science and so on.

 The models I worked on include

  • Large Language Models
  • Dimensionality reduction
  • Manifold learning
  • Robust models
  • Blind source separation
  • Subspace clustering

 These methodologies have been applied to many problems in spectroscopy, remote sensing, computer vision, image processing, signal processing and so on.

 My recent research focuses on data science including 

  • Natural Language Processing (NLP) modeling
  • spatial temporal models (for tensorial data) 
  • complex neural networks 
  • Neural processing

If you are interested in pursuing PhD study in machine learning or computational statistics, please contact me through email.

HDR Supervision

I am currently supervising and co-supervising PhD and HDR students in the areas of machine learning, computational statistics, and data science. My students are working on various research topics, including:
